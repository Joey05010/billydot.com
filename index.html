About Me

I love design, and I love what data can tell me about it. I'm intrigued by how people interact with the things around them: where they pause, what they skip, what they reach for first. Most of my ideas start there.

I've worked in almost every field of design, from UX and product to graphic design, motion and web. Storytelling and creativity tie it all together for me. A good design should tell its own story, and I enjoy finding the clearest way to tell it.

AI fascinates me, and I'm always looking for the best ways to bring it into my work, to test ideas sooner and spend more time on the parts that need a human eye.

I work well with others, especially developers. The best products come from designers and engineers solving problems together, and that's where I like to be.
